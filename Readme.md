## 🚀 Overview

Welcome to our Ride Sharing App, where we are transforming the way people experience urban transportation. While ride-hailing services are commonplace, our app introduces exciting new features designed to make your ride experience more seamless, secure, and enjoyable. Whether you're heading to work, the airport, or just out for a ride, our app ensures you get to your destination with ease and comfort.

What sets us apart?

- AI-Powered Ride Matching: The app intelligently matches you with the best driver based on your location, ride preferences, and driver ratings.
- Real-Time Ride Tracking: Track your ride from start to finish, ensuring you’re always in the loop about your driver’s location and estimated arrival time.
- Driver Ratings & Reviews: Get real-time feedback from passengers and share your own experience to ensure only the best drivers are on the road.
- Payment Flexibility: Secure, fast, and flexible payment options make it easy to pay for your ride, from credit cards to mobile wallets.
- Sleek, User-Friendly Interface: The app is designed with a clean and simple interface that allows you to book your ride, view driver details, and track your journey—all at the touch of a button.

## ✨ Features

-🚗 AI-Driven Ride Matching: The app learns your ride preferences, ensuring the best possible match for every journey.
- 🛣️ Real-Time Ride Tracking: Follow your driver in real-time with live updates to stay informed during your journey.
- ⭐ Driver Ratings & Reviews: Check driver ratings, leave your feedback, and improve the overall experience for all users.
- 💳 Multiple Payment Options: Pay easily and securely with your preferred payment method.
- 🕹️ In-App Ride Cancellation: Quickly cancel your ride with a simple tap, in case your plans change.
- 🌍 Multi-City Support: The app is available in various cities, allowing you to request rides anywhere you go.

## 🛠 Installation

```sh
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/yourusername/ride-sharing-app.git
cd ride-sharing-app

# Install dependencies
# For the backend, run:
cd backend
npm install

# For the frontend, run:
cd frontend
npm install

# Run the Application
# Backend Setup
cd backend
npm start

# Frontend Setup
cd frontend
npm start
```

## 📚  Learning Goals
This project provides a fantastic learning opportunity by:

- Developing a real-time, location-based ride-sharing platform with integrated maps and driver tracking.
- Understanding the backend and frontend integration in a real-world application.
- Exploring secure payment integration and real-time notifications.
- Gaining hands-on experience in managing real-time data with Socket.io.

## 🎯 Issues & Limitations
🚧 Limited Feature Set: Although the app includes basic ride-booking features, future updates may bring expanded functionalities such as ride scheduling, carpooling, and advanced surge pricing algorithms.

🚧 Mobile Optimization: The app works on mobile, but further optimization for specific devices may be required in the future.
